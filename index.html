Written Statement

For the company record. Anyone on the shift can fill this out. It takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

  • Write only what you saw or heard yourself.
  • Write it in your own words. Spanish or English, whichever is easier.
  • If you did not see it, did not hear it, or do not remember, say so. "I don't know" is a good answer.
  • Do not write what you think someone felt or meant. Write what they did and said.
  • Write your statement before you talk it over with other people.

Please write your full name.

2. What is your statement about?

Check every one that fits.

Please check at least one.

If you are describing more than one day, leave this empty and put the date at the start of each part of your statement.

3. How do you know this?

Check every one that fits.

Please check at least one.

4. Your statement

Tell it in order, from the beginning. Include:

  • What was happening just before.
  • What each person did and said. Who was standing where.
  • How loud, and who could hear it.
  • What happened right after.

Please write your statement.

Write them in the language they were said, in quotes, and say who said them.

Say what you have and who has it. Do not attach anything here. Matt will ask for it.

Please check the box to sign.

Please type your full name.
