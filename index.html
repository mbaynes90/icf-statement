Written Statement

Anyone on the shift can fill this out. It takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

  • Write only what you saw or heard yourself.
  • Use your own words, in Spanish or English, whichever is easier.
  • Include what you saw or heard whether it helps or hurts any person involved. Do not leave something out because it may upset someone.
  • If you did not see it, did not hear it, or do not remember, say so. "I don't know" is a good answer.
  • Write what people did and said. Do not guess what they felt or meant.
  • Write your statement before you talk it over with other people.

This statement is voluntary. It is used by the company to review what happened. It may be shared with HR and with the company's advisers, and with an agency if the law requires. Please keep it confidential. Giving an honest statement will not be held against you.
